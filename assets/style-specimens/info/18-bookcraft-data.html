In This year Echo Records took in from the neighbourhood 1,248 used vinyl records, of which 312 came in whole boxes: a retired radio producer brought thirty years of records over in three trips.

Sold 1,091 records. The fastest spent forty minutes on the shelf; the slowest waited four hundred and two days, and the buyer said he had walked past this street many times.

Listen The shop's listening deck played 6,430 times. After 8 pm on Fridays, the old turntable was busier than the till.

We did no growth marketing and no membership. Regulars: 87 , the number of people we can call by name.

The priciest record was a 4,800 first-pressing jazz LP, sold to a young man who paid after seeing only the sleeve.

Next year open up the second floor, fit shelves for 2,000 records, and leave one wall empty.
