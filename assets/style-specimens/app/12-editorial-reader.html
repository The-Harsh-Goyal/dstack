Why regulars never look at the tea menu

Lin Su · 8 min · 62% read

At one in the morning old Zhou pushed the door open without a word; the keeper had already set his pot on the table by the window.

The menu here isn't for ordering; it's for confirming "you haven't been forgotten". New customers only find out on page three that prices have never gone up.

The teahouse has few rules; one of them: whoever speaks first refills the water.

The rule has one upside: nobody ever falls silent.
