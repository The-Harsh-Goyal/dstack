City bus · 2026 network optimization review

After merging overlapping routes, rush-hour average waits can drop by a quarter

A review of ridership and arrival data on 38 trunk routes shows 11 overlap more than 60% on the east-west corridor: buses bunch at peak, while adjacent periods have gaps of 20+ minutes.

We propose merging these 11 into 5, with the same fleet size, and reallocating the saved headway to transfer hubs.
