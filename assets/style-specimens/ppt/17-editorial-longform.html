We turned "protection" from a verb into a ledger anyone can check

Chair Lin Zhao · January 2026

Three years ago, at our first interview in a fishing harbour, an old captain asked: that sea you protect, can I go and see it? That question became the standard for all our work.

In 2025 we stopped reporting results as "area covered". Every protected stretch of sea now maps to a public monitoring record: who measured what and when, where the data is, and when the next re-measurement is.

The result surprised us a little. Donors ask more detailed questions, and partner fishing villages are more willing to share their data, because they know the numbers will come back to them.
