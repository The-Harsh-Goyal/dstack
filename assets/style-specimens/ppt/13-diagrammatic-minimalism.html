Market redesign · method

A good market grows outward from its centre

Most redesigns start at the outer ring: new stalls, matching signs. We go the other way: first ask why this place needs a market, then decide how it runs, and only last what it looks like.
Case comparison
Dongfeng Rd market was redone from the outside in: 31% vacancy two years later; Locust Lane market started from the centre: 4% vacancy.
