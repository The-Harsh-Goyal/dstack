We pay wages for one thing only:
that the customer goes home with a record he'll play a second time.

Not selling the most, not the busiest shop. We call it the "second-spin test": next time this customer comes back, if he says "I played that one again", you did right this week.

So we don't push stock. However much stock piles up, it can't be traded for a customer's ears.
