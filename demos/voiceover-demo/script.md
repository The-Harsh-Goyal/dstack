## intro
Have you ever wondered, [[cue:question]]when we talk to an AI, how the AI actually understands what we say?

## token-1
The answer is that it doesn't read words at all. [[cue:reveal]]It only knows tokens.

## token-2
You can think of a token as the AI's smallest unit of information.
For example, the word "playground" [[cue:split]]may be two tokens to an AI: play and ground.

## ending
So next time you see a phrase like "million-token context", [[cue:context]]you'll know it means how many of these little chunks the AI can remember at once.
